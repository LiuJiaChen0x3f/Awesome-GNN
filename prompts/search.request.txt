Topic eligibility:
- Include original research with a concrete new learning, adaptation, or inference method. Exclude surveys, reviews, tutorials, benchmarks without a proposed method, and position papers even when their topic is TAG or OOD.
- The primary method or task must address TAG (text-attributed graphs) OR OOD (out-of-distribution graph learning). These are the ONLY active topic labels; generic GNN, SSL, CL, TGN, KG or other graph topics do not independently qualify.
- TAG requires meaningful learning over graphs whose nodes or edges carry natural-language text, with a method that uses text semantics and graph structure. Using an LLM or a knowledge graph alone is insufficient.
- OOD requires an explicit graph train/test distribution shift, cross-domain adaptation/generalization, or environment-shift problem with relevant method design/evaluation. Ordinary random-split accuracy, few-shot learning, temporal prediction or generic robustness alone is insufficient.
- For OOD, the learned representations and predictions must operate on graph-structured data as graph learning. Language-model reasoning over a textual description of a toy graph, such as reachability questions from edge sentences, is not graph-learning OOD even when graph depth changes between training and testing.
- Use OR semantics: one of TAG/OOD is enough; both are allowed. Background mentions are insufficient. Full-text evidence must support each assigned topic; never relabel an unrelated paper to fill quotas.
