Find recent graph-learning papers within the configured date window, prioritizing the most recently published eligible papers.

Venue eligibility:
- Include CCF-A main-conference papers and EMNLP main-conference papers. EMNLP is the user's sole permitted CCF-B exception; this does not admit other B-class conferences.
- Independently include papers with a verified arXiv record, regardless of conference rank or acceptance status. No conference acceptance is required for the arXiv route. A paper with an arXiv version remains eligible even if it also appears in another conference, Findings, a workshop, or a journal.
- For papers without a verified arXiv record, exclude journals, workshops, standalone book chapters, Findings of EMNLP, other preprints without verified eligible conference acceptance, and all other ineligible venues. A main-conference proceedings paper is not excluded merely because a metadata service encodes it as a book chapter.
- Verify the actual conference and track from authoritative proceedings or publisher metadata. Do not infer acceptance from the title, abstract, author affiliation, or an unverified arXiv record. Do not infer CCF rank from the existing broad venue configuration.

Topic eligibility:
- The primary method or task must involve at least one of these 11 controlled keywords in a graph-learning context: GNN, SSL, CL, OOD, TAG, GSL, HGT, TGN, KG, KGE, HGNN.
- Use OR semantics: one matching topic is sufficient; several are allowed. Do not require all 11 or favor an older paper simply because it matches more topics. Mere background mentions are insufficient.

Source quotas (hard requirement):
- Split the requested result count evenly: ceil(N/2) independently verified conference papers and floor(N/2) independently verified arXiv papers. For 10 results, return 5 conference + 5 arXiv; for 3, return 2 conference + 1 arXiv. Runtime source_quotas is authoritative.
- Conference slots require an eligible primary proceedings record; arXiv slots require an arXiv record and do not depend on conference rank or acceptance. An arXiv page mentioning a conference is not enough to fill a conference slot.
- Deduplicate globally by DOI, arXiv ID and normalized title. A paper found through both routes may occupy either eligible bucket, but never two slots. Retain enough distinct candidates to meet both quotas.
- Search deficient buckets explicitly and prioritize missing conference slots when arXiv is already full. Never fill one bucket's shortfall with the other. If the window or verification budget prevents filling a quota, return the partial results and report the shortfall separately for each bucket.

Recency and ordering:
- Search both the newest eligible proceedings and the newest arXiv submissions; request descending publication or first-submission date order where supported. Select newest eligible papers within each source quota, deduplicating globally; then display the combined selection newest first. The quotas take precedence over a global newest-N ranking.
- Use the verified formal publication date of the eligible conference paper, preferring the publisher's first online publication date for that conference version, then the proceedings publication date. Keep an earlier preprint submission date separate.
- For the arXiv route, use the first submission date (v1), not the latest revision date. For a deduplicated paper with both routes, retain both dates and their provenance. Use the date of the route assigned to its quota for this run: conference publication for conference slots, arXiv first submission for arXiv slots. Both routes must independently satisfy the date window to be eligible for either assignment. Return the paper only once.
- Do not substitute revision, indexing, crawling, metadata-update, or acceptance dates for publication dates. Do not let a new revision make an old paper appear newly published.
- Filter by the configured publication-date window and order eligible results by publication date, newest first. Use topic relevance only to break date ties.
- Preserve partial date precision; never invent a month or day. If a missing or partial date cannot establish window eligibility or exact relative order, flag the uncertainty and do not claim the paper is among the newest verified results. Do not silently widen the window or relax venue constraints to fill the result count.

Output:
- Return at most the runtime requested_count with the required source split; fewer verified results are acceptable only with explicit source-specific shortages.
- In the final webpage, show only the verified conference abbreviation (for example, NeurIPS, ICML, KDD, or EMNLP) in the conference badge, never its full name or a journal label.
- For papers included through the arXiv route without a verified eligible conference, use the badge "arXiv"; do not invent a conference or rank. If an eligible conference is verified, use its abbreviation and retain the arXiv link as a source/PDF link.
